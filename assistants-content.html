Подробно (11 бележки)
  • Opened to all users on 19 Oct 2026 ('Wabi 2.0 is live!'), after an invite-only start on 28 Sep 2026
  • Works like a messenger with several agentic threads for different tasks and long-term goals; each thread can hold custom apps such as trackers, calendars or itineraries
